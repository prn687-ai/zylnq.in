Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Zylnq Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Zylnq.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information: • Profile information (name, photo, job title, skills, location) • Account credentials and authentication data • Usage data (swipes, matches, interactions) • Device information (device type, OS, app version) • Location data (if you grant permission) • Communications between users

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: • Provide and personalise the Zylnq service • Match you with relevant professionals • Send notifications and updates • Improve our AI recommendation engine • Prevent fraud and ensure platform safety • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with: • Other users (only your public profile information) • Service providers who operate under strict confidentiality agreements • Law enforcement when required by law We never share your data with advertisers without your explicit consent.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers hosted in India. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL) for data in transit and AES-256 for data at rest. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only.

5. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to: • Access your personal data • Correct inaccurate data • Erase your data (processed within 72 hours) • Withdraw consent at any time • Nominate a representative for your data To exercise these rights, contact: dpo@zylnq.in

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use analytics tools to understand app usage patterns. This data is anonymised and aggregated. You can opt out of analytics tracking in the app settings under Privacy.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, your personal data is erased within 72 hours. Anonymised usage data may be retained for analytics purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Zylnq is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Grievance Officer

For privacy-related complaints or queries, contact our Grievance Officer at grievance@zylnq.in. We will respond within 24 hours and resolve within 15 days.
