How it works

Three steps to your next opportunity

No cold emails. No unanswered LinkedIn requests. Just meaningful connections — fast.

1

Build your profile

Add your skills, experience, goals, and what you're open to — hiring, mentoring, collaborating, or fundraising.

2

Swipe to connect

Our AI surfaces the most relevant professionals for your goals. Swipe right to express interest, left to pass.

3

Match & chat

When both parties connect, a conversation opens. No awkward cold reach-outs — both sides opted in.

4

Grow your network

Attend events, post opportunities, refer candidates, and build your professional reputation over time.
