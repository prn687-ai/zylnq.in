Built for India's
ambitious professionals.

India has millions of talented engineers, designers, founders, investors, and marketers — all looking for the right connection. But finding the right people has always been slow, awkward, and luck-based.

Zylnq changes that. Tell us your goals, and our AI surfaces the most relevant people in your city and industry — instantly. Swipe to connect. Match to chat. Build together.

Your goals.
Your network.

Whether you're a founder seeking a co-founder, a recruiter scouting top talent, a fresher finding a mentor, or a freelancer growing their client base — Zylnq has a place for you.

Beyond matching, Zylnq brings your professional world together: job opportunities, industry events, candidate referrals, and a community that grows with you.

How it works

Four steps to your
next opportunity

No cold messages. No unanswered requests. Just intentional, warm connections — fast.

1

Build your profile

Add your skills, experience, city, and goals. The more specific you are, the stronger your matches will be.

2

Swipe to connect

Our AI surfaces the most relevant professionals for your goals. Swipe right to connect, left to pass.

3

Match & chat

When both parties connect, a chat opens. Both opted in — so every conversation starts warm.

4

Grow & achieve

Attend events, post opportunities, refer candidates, and build your professional reputation over time.
